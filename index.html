How it works

1

Paste or Upload

Enter a single title or upload a CSV, with row limits based on your plan: 100 rows for Guest, 1,000 for Basic, and 10,000 for Pro.

2

Structured Classification

The engine cleans each title and suggests domain, seniority, work nature, and confidence flags for your review.

3

Export Results

Download your classified dataset as CSV or Excel, ready for review, reporting, or downstream analysis. JSON export also available.

4

Analyse & Ask

View auto-generated charts and export summaries as PNG or PDF where available. Pro users can also ask the AI Assistant questions about their data.

Beta Notice

Logititles is currently in beta. Plan limits, pricing, and available features may change as the product continues to develop. Fair use limits may apply during beta to protect system performance.

Data Privacy

Please avoid uploading confidential, personal, or sensitive company data. Use sample, public, or anonymised data where possible. Uploaded data should only be used for testing, cleaning, and classification purposes.

Feature Availability

Some features including AI-assisted insights, salary reference fields, and advanced analytics are currently in beta and may be refined over time. Salary fields are reference information only and should not be treated as official benchmarks.

Affiliation Disclaimer

Logititles is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with SEEK, Indeed, LINZ, or any external job platform. Any job title data used for testing should come from public, sample, or user-provided datasets.
